# Acme Corp — Incident Response

## Purpose

This document explains how Acme Corp responds to security incidents.

## Reporting

If you see a security incident, report it to IT.

## Response

The IT team will investigate and respond appropriately. Serious incidents will be escalated to management.

## Customers

If customers are affected, we will let them know.

## Lessons learned

After major incidents, the team will review what happened and try to do better next time.
